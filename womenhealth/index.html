            Method and limits

            Special issues, themed series and collections on women's health published from January 2024 to October 2026 were found on journal and publisher pages across general medicine, neurology and psychiatry, menopause and OB/GYN, endocrinology, cardiology, public health and open-access platforms. Each article title was tagged with topics by keyword matching (the full keyword list is below); an article can carry several topics.

            Issue weight gives every issue equal weight, split across its articles, so a single 100-plus-article collection does not dominate. Open calls are tagged from their published scope text.

            Limits: titles only, not abstracts, so broad editorials are under-tagged; the trends describe what editors chose for dedicated issues, not the whole literature; 2026 is a partial year; several publisher sites (The Lancet, JAMA, ScienceDirect, AHA journals) could not be read automatically, so some issues are incomplete or missing.
